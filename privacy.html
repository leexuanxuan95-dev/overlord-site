Overlord — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-26

Overlord does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

All habit data, check-ins, streaks, and boss quotes are stored exclusively on your device and never leave it. There is no account system, no cloud sync, no analytics SDK, no third-party tracking, and no advertising.

What we don't collect

We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, IP addresses, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other personal or device information.

Network access

Overlord makes zero network calls during normal use. The app works fully offline, including in airplane mode. Boss quotes are assembled on-device from pre-approved templates. No content is fetched from any server.

In-App Purchases

Purchases of additional boss characters are handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes payment, not us. We receive no information about your Apple ID, payment method, or billing address. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Children

Overlord is rated 17+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

Data you delete from the app (via Settings → Reset all data, or by uninstalling the app) is permanently destroyed. We hold no backup of your data, because we never received it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be posted at this URL.
