General

Is this a joke?

Half. The app really does bench you if you miss three days — your boss goes silent, your streak resets, and you have to tap "I'm Back" to get reinstated. The humor is the delivery, not the mechanic.

Is the harsh tone safe for everyone?

Probably not. Overlord is satire, rated 17+, and uses an aggressive coaching style. If a strict tone genuinely stresses you out, this app isn't for you — try Finch, Streaks, or Way of Life. We're not for everyone, and we're fine with that.

Can I soften the tone?

Yes. Settings → Gentle Mode filters out the harshest language while keeping the accountability mechanic. You can toggle it any time, including from the first-launch disclaimer.

Does it use AI?

No. Zero network calls. All boss replies are algorithmically assembled on-device from pre-approved templates — over three million combinations. Works in airplane mode.

Purchases

Is it a subscription?

No. One-time purchase. The first boss is free. Additional bosses are $2.99 each, or $9.99 for the full bundle. No hidden fees, no recurring charges.

How do refunds work?

Apple handles refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com within 14 days of purchase. We don't hold the money ourselves.

Restore purchases?

Settings → Restore Purchases. Uses your Apple ID — works on any device signed in to the same account.

Data & Privacy

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no server, no cloud sync, no account. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What happens if I delete the app?

Everything is gone. Streak, rank, history, purchases of bosses can be restored via Apple ID — but local data cannot. Back up by screenshotting your Career and Quote History pages.

Can I export my data?

Not in v1.0. Coming in a future update.

Devices

Why iOS only?

Single developer. Android is on the roadmap, no ETA.

iPad / Apple Watch / Mac?

iPhone and Apple Watch are supported. iPad runs in scaled iPhone mode. Mac Catalyst is not supported in v1.

Does the widget work offline?

Yes. The widget reads local data only.

Troubleshooting

The app won't open / keeps crashing

Force-quit the app (swipe up from the app switcher), then reopen. If the crash persists, restart your iPhone. Still broken? Email support@overlord.app with your iOS version and device model.

I missed a day, but the app didn't bench me

The 3-day clock starts after your last successful check-in. If you opened the app and tapped "I'm Back" at any point in the last 3 days, the counter reset.

Notifications aren't appearing

Settings (iOS) → Notifications → Overlord → make sure all toggles are on. Inside the app, Settings → Notifications → confirm scheduling is enabled.

Disclaimer

Overlord is fiction. The "boss" characters are not real people. The "BENCHED" mechanic is a deliberate satire of corporate-style accountability and not intended as actual psychological pressure. The app is for adults who understand the joke. If you're in genuine emotional distress, please reach out to a licensed professional or call your local crisis line.
